# IQ2 source notice

The IQ2 signed-grid table, codecs and native quantization helpers derive from
DS4/ggml at the upstream base recorded in `docs/halo/sources.json`. They are
covered by the repository MIT license, including the notices for the DS4
and ggml authors and DeepSeek. The frozen S9 producers and descriptor mapping
are retained with renamed entry points and architecture registration guards.
HIP and rocWMMA remain separately licensed external dependencies.
